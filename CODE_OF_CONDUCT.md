# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, safe, and respectful environment
for everyone participating in the Health project, regardless of experience,
identity, background, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Welcome questions and different perspectives.
- Give feedback about ideas and code, not people.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.
- Protect other people's privacy and avoid sharing personal or health
  information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized attention,
deliberate intimidation, and publishing another person's private information
without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

The repository owner, `@xpelch`, is responsible for clarifying and enforcing
this code. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate, threatening,
or harmful.

Raise a project conduct concern with `@xpelch` in the relevant GitHub
conversation when it is safe to do so. Do not put private or sensitive details
in a public issue. Use GitHub's reporting and blocking features for abuse that
requires a private platform-level report or an immediate safety response.

Security vulnerabilities must be reported through the separate private process
in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces and when someone officially represents
the project in public.
